\section{Conclusion}\label{sec:conclusion}

We proposed SAKI, which combines sample-level relation selection, constrained knowledge verbalization, and Hierarchical Fusion to rerank candidate classes without updating CLAP parameters. Across five public test datasets, SAKI improved Hit@1 by 2.08--12.73 percentage points and MRR by 1.02--6.68 percentage points over iKnow\textsuperscript{\dag}. It also achieved the highest unweighted mean Hit@1 and MRR in the component ablations. \redrevision{Under the evaluated settings, these findings support coordinating sample relevance, text representation, and score fusion when incorporating structured knowledge into pretrained audio--language models.} Future work will examine evidence reliability and lightweight relation selection, and assess applicability across different audio--language models and knowledge graphs.
